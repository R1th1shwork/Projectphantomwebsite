Questions

Asked, answered.

01What is Vector?+

Vector is an invite-only collective in the UAE for students who want to point themselves at aerospace, artificial intelligence and robotics. Small by design, led by students, entered by nomination or a written application.

02Who leads Vector?+

The same student team behind Project Phantom. They built award-winning aircraft as teenagers, changed their own direction doing it, and now vector other students the way they vectored themselves.

03How do I apply to Vector?+

A member nominates you, or you apply in writing below. Name, email, phone, date of birth and a document on what you like, what you do and why you want in, either typed or as a photograph of a handwritten letter. Most applications are declined.

04Do I have to be into aerospace?+

No. Vector covers aerospace, artificial intelligence and robotics, and the work is real hardware and real software. If you build things, you fit.
