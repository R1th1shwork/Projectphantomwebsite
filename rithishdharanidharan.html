About

INDEX / 01

I build things that have to survive the air — airframes, flight logic, and the systems underneath them.

Based in the UAE, I've spent the past couple of years turning a school project into Project Phantom: an autonomous UAV platform built for environmental monitoring, from aerodynamic design and rapid prototyping through to simulation and real-world flight testing. Outside of aircraft, I work across applied AI, embedded systems, and healthcare and energy technology — including AEGIS, a diagnostic support system, and LiCycle, a self-healing battery concept that placed first at the WFES AI Energy Hackathon.

I'm currently the Artificial Intelligence Ambassador at Gulf Asian English School and part of the Tech & AI Committee at Unipreneur Inc., alongside continuing to fly, break, and rebuild Phantom.
