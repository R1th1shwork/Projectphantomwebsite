01Profile

Engineering with a deadline attached.

My work sits where hardware, software and a real test decide whether an idea survives. Project Phantom started as a design question about vertical take-off and now runs flight campaigns, a custom flight stack and an onboard AI layer.

Outside Phantom I build applied-AI systems for energy, healthcare and emergency response, and I spend the rest of my time in programmes that put the work in front of people who can judge it: hackathons, national competitions and technical cohorts.

02Systems

What I have built.

Five builds, each starting from a constraint rather than a concept.

01

Project Phantom

A single-rotor fixed-wing VTOL aircraft for environmental monitoring, running electric propulsion, solar-assisted endurance and the onboard Mercury AI layer. I lead platform direction and flight-system development; four test flights logged 28 minutes of atmospheric data up to 150 metres, and the platform placed third at the Dubai Award for Sustainable Transport in 2026.

Founder / flight systems projectphantom.space ↗
02

LiCycle

A self-healing battery system that uses sensor data and AI analytics to catch cell failure before it takes useful energy offline. Built with a team at the World Future Energy Summit AI Energy Hackathon, where it took first place.

1st, WFES hackathon
03

One Force

A disaster-response concept coordinating drones for detection, assistance and communications relay where connectivity has failed. Won Overall Champion and Best Social Impact at AppSmart 2025.

Overall champion, AppSmart
04

AEGIS

Deep learning applied to endoscopic imaging to surface earlier gastrointestinal-disease signals. The project placed third in the STEM & AI Innovators' Challenge at PACEGEN 2025.

3rd, PACEGEN 2025
05

VIPER

A vision system that identifies faulty pipe fittings on a moving conveyor and triggers a physical rejection, closing the loop from camera input to machine action rather than stopping at a model output.

Prototype / automation
