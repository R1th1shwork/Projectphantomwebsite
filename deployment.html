Technology Transfer

UAE Knowledge
to the World

Beyond hardware export, this covers capacity building, joint ventures, and training programs, positioning the UAE as a center for solar UAV expertise.

01
Manufacturing License
Regional manufacturing partners licensed to produce Persistent Core and wing modules. Quality standards maintained. IP protected.
02
Operator Training
Certified training program for local operators in partner nations. Abu Dhabi-based academy. 40-hour ground school + 20-hour flight certification.
03
MERCURY Integration
Local language AI interface. Country-specific environmental databases. National regulatory compliance built into flight envelope.
04
Climate Data Sharing
Global environmental data consortium. Partner nations contribute and receive shared intelligence network. Science without borders.
