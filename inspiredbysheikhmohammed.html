Unipreneur / built in Dubai

A platform for young people ready to build what is next.

Unipreneur is a Dubai-based youth entrepreneurship, intrapreneurship and innovation development platform. It brings young people closer to the skills, industry experience and opportunities that help future leaders move from idea to action.
