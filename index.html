One of UAE's first youth-led initiatives in autonomous fixed-wing aerospace

ONE UAV. INFINITE MISSIONS.

Project Phantom is a single-rotor VTOL aircraft that takes off and lands vertically, then transitions to fixed-wing flight for efficient cruise. No runway is required. Solar cells integrated into the wing extend endurance, and the platform runs entirely on electric propulsion.

Overview

How Phantom Works

Most UAVs force a tradeoff between range, payload, and versatility. Phantom's modular architecture removes that tradeoff.

Persistent Core
One standardized fuselage carries flight control, power management, AI computing, and communications.
Single-Engine VTOL
Vertical takeoff and landing with zero runway required, transitioning to efficient fixed-wing glide for cruise.
Solar Integration
Solar cells integrated into the wing surface reduce battery drain during cruise.

Configurable Wing System

One architecture adapted for every environment.
